# jenson_dataset_sql

💼 Project Showcase – Retail Data Analysis at Jensen’s

 📊 Advanced SQL-Driven Insights for Business Strategy

I recently completed a comprehensive data analytics project focused on retail operations for Jenson’s, a multi-store retail chain. The objective was to derive meaningful insights from raw transactional data and recommend strategies to enhance business performance.

🎯 Project Objective

To analyze transactional, operational, and customer data using SQL and deliver insights that inform decisions around:

🛒 Customer behavior and purchasing patterns
👨‍💼 Staff performance and sales contributions
📦 Inventory efficiency and stock movement
🏬 Store-level performance and optimization opportunities

By combining analytical techniques with business thinking, I aimed to bridge the gap between data and strategy.

📌 Key Findings and SQL-Backed Insights

✅ Sales Trends
 • Identified top-performing products and categories by revenue and volume
 • Tracked cumulative product sales over time using window functions
 • Detected unsold inventory for strategic markdowns and promotions

✅ Customer Segmentation
 • Pinpointed high-value customers based on total spending
 • Identified customers purchasing across all categories (category coverage)
 • Mapped customer activity across stores for targeted campaigns

✅ Staff Performance
 • Flagged sales staff exceeding performance benchmarks
 • Isolated staff with no recorded sales for potential upskilling

✅ Operational Intelligence
 • Uncovered category-level pricing insights including medians and anomalies
 • Highlighted store-wise order volumes to support resource allocation

🧠 Recommended Strategic Actions

Inventory Optimization: Reallocate or bundle stagnant products

Customer Retention: Launch loyalty programs for high-LTV customers

Performance Management: Support underperforming staff through training

Category Management: Adjust pricing based on median and sales patterns

🧰 Tech Stack
SQL (MySQL) – Advanced subqueries, CTEs, window functions, EXISTS, RANK, PARTITION BY, and more

Data Modelling & Insight Generation – Translating business requirements 
 into analytical logic

Data Storytelling – Turning insights into stakeholder-ready narratives
